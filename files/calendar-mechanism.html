All wheels turning — no alignment at the pointers.

Sun — one turn per tropical year; the bold mark is the winter solstice, the small marks the equinoxes and summer solstice. Moon — one turn per lunation; bold mark full moon, small marks the lunar quarters. Rhyme — one turn per 19 years; nineteen teeth, one per year of the Metonic beat. Shadow — one turn per eclipse year of 346.62 days; its two marks are the dragon's head and tail, the lunar nodes. When sun, moon, and shadow marks stand together at their pointers, a full moon meets the Earth's shadow on the longest night: the age turns.
